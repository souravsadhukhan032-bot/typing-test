# Logo assets

## Generic illustrations

The SVG files in the numbered folders are illustrative placeholders, not official agency or department insignia. They are retained for the logo gallery only and must not be presented as authentic government logos or as copyright-free assets.

## Sourced department logos used by the site

The exam pages use local copies in `official/` only for agencies with an available image from an official department website. Logos remain the property of their respective organizations; check each department's usage terms before redistributing them.
